Patient Information

Cardiology Care FAQ

When should I seek consultation with a cardiologist?

We recommend consulting a cardiologist if you experience symptoms like persistent chest pain, shortness of breath, unexplained dizziness, palpitations, or if you possess high risk factors including chronic high blood pressure, diabetes, or a heavy family history of cardiac events.

What tests do you perform at Echelon Hospital?

We feature fully functional digital electrocardiography (ECG), high-resolution echocardiography, treadmill stress tests (TMT), Holter dynamic monitors, and state-of-the-art coronary angiography within our critical catheterization labs.

Do I need a prior referral to request an appointment?

No direct referral is necessary. You can self-schedule or request a priority consultation directly through our online request system or by contacting our patient services coordinator.

Do you accept my health insurance provider?

Echelon Hospital maintains direct billing and in-network agreements with all major national health insurance providers. Our financial coordinators will assist in processing pre-authorizations smoothly.
